01 Programs

Four ways
to train here

Every programme is capped by rack count and coach count — never by how many people we can fit through the door. You book a slot, you get the equipment you came for.

New here?

Your first session is a 50-minute assessment: movement screen, strength baselines, and a programme written for your week — not a generic 7-day pass.

01

Strength & Powerlifting

Barbell-first programming on calibrated plates and competition bars. Squat, bench, deadlift and press with a coach checking your setup on every heavy set.

90 minMax 8 / slotMon–Sat
02

Conditioning & Engine

Rowing, sled, assault bike and threshold intervals, re-tested every four weeks. You'll see the numbers move on a sheet, not just feel tired.

50 minMax 12 / slot6× weekly
03

Boxing & Combat Conditioning

Pads, heavy bags, footwork ladders and core work under fatigue. Technique blocks before intensity — beginners are not thrown into a conditioning grinder on day one.

60 minMax 16 / slot4× weekly
04

Mobility & Recovery

Loaded stretches, soft-tissue work and guided breathing, plus sauna and cold plunge access. Built for the desk-heavy, the post-injury and the over-40 lifters.

40 minMax 10 / slotDaily
