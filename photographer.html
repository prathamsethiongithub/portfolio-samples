About

I photograph the in-between parts.

I started in 2013 as a second shooter for a studio in Bandra, carrying bags and learning how to stand very still. Thirteen years later I still work quietly — the best frame of the day is usually taken somewhere between the four that were planned.

Most of my couples tell me they hate being photographed. So the work is built around not asking much of you: a walk at golden hour, a shared cup of chai, twenty minutes alone on the mehndi floor. My assistant and I shoot two cameras, film and digital, and we never take over your day — not for the light, not for the shot list, not for anyone's feed.

Everything you receive is edited by me, in my own studio. Nothing is outsourced, and nothing is delivered twice-looking-the-same. A wedding takes about six weeks to finish; an album takes twelve.
